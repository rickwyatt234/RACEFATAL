# Focus camera and deployed mine marker

Hold the existing focus control (Ctrl) to highlight the selected racer with cyan corner brackets and show that racer’s ID. Tab cycles focus targets. The cockpit camera smoothly turns toward the target and narrows its field of view. Releasing focus, losing a valid target, or disabling the targeting HUD clears focus and restores the normal view; boost still uses its regular field of view. The camera stays mounted to the cockpit. Destruction clears focus before the death camera takes over.

On PlayerCockpitView, Focus View exposes Focus Field Of View (default 45 degrees) and Focus Look Response (default 7). The existing Field Of View Response controls zoom smoothing. On PlayerCockpitTargetingHUD, Focus Highlight exposes bracket color and size in HUD canvas units. Brackets are generated at runtime beside the existing ID box or rocket reticle, so no prefab wiring is needed. The stabilized windshield projection compensates its size during zoom, and focus markers project after the camera’s final pose.

On Assets/Game/Scripts/Presentation/WeaponPresentation/WeaponPresentation_BallisticMine.asset, assign the looping glow prefab to **Deployed Mine Marker → Deployed Mine VFX Prefab**. Adjust Deployed Mine VFX Offset and Scale to fit it. Particle systems start on deployment; Visual Effect Graph prefabs should use their normal on-enable event. Author the effect to loop and avoid a self-destruction script. The marker is a child of the mine and is removed when it detonates, expires, or the race ends. Impact Prefab remains the detonation effect. Without a marker prefab, the existing pulsing point light remains; the mine sphere now uses an HDRP-compatible emissive material.
